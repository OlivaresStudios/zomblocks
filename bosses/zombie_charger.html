Wiki / Bosses / The Mutated

The Mutated

BOSSNEVER INFECTS

A brute with a huge armoured arm (1.9 times bigger than a zombie). It charges in a straight line and pins survivors against the walls.

Its lair: Brick Factory

It waits in its lair and wakes up when you step onto the arena floor. Fight on the top floor. Your guidebook radio leads you there. All the lairs →

Boss bar

Shown over its head during the fight, in its own theme.

Attacks

Arm smash

Slams its armoured arm 2.4 blocks in front of it: everyone within 4.6 blocks is hit.

12 dmg
Charge

From 6 to 22 blocks away, it rushes in a straight line and tackles everyone in its path. At most every 7 s.

11 dmg
Call the team

Calls 2 team members every 27 s (never more than 6 around it).

x2

Phases

Stunned

If its charge ends in a wall, it is stunned for 3.5 s and takes double damage.

Armor

Its arm absorbs 25% of every hit (it takes 75%), x2 while stunned. It never infects.

Team

The zombies it calls into the fight, 2 at a time.

Loot

Every player who hurt it gets their own loot in their inventory: 20 to 30 scrap, 1 jackpot and 2 different bonus items. 25% chance that a weapon comes upgraded (a random mod, or Reinforced III).

HOW TO BEAT IT
  • Stand in front of a wall when it charges and jump aside at the last moment: it crashes and is stunned.
  • Hit it as hard as you can during the stun: every hit counts double.
  • Do not stay in a long corridor with it: the charge covers 22 blocks.
