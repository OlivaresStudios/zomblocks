The Mech Trooper

BOSSNEVER INFECTS

A zombie in a mech suit, about 4 blocks tall, with a rocket launcher, a grappling claw and a jetpack. It fights from every distance.

Its lair: Fuel Depot

It waits in its lair and wakes up when you step onto the arena floor. Fight between the tanks. Your guidebook radio leads you there. All the lairs →

Boss bar

Shown over its head during the fight, in its own theme.

Attacks

Rocket salvo

Fires 3 rockets in a row from 4 to 24 blocks away. Each one explodes (3 blocks wide).

7 dmg each
Grappling claw

From 2.5 to 9 blocks: the claw pulls you to it, then it punches you.

8 dmg
Jetpack slam

Flies to you from 5 to 16 blocks away and crashes down: everyone within 4 blocks is hit and thrown back.

10 dmg
Call the team

Calls 2 team members every 27 s (never more than 6 around it).

x2

Armor

The suit absorbs 45% of every hit (it takes 55%, with sparks), but explosions deal 140%. It never infects.

Team

The zombies it calls into the fight, 2 at a time.

Soldier BruteRocketeerThe Demolisher

Loot

Every player who hurt it gets their own loot in their inventory: 20 to 30 scrap, 1 jackpot and 2 different bonus items. 25% chance that a weapon comes upgraded (a random mod, or Reinforced III).

HOW TO BEAT IT
  • There is no safe distance: rockets far, claw mid-range, jetpack in between. Use cover against the rockets.
  • Explosives are by far the best answer to its armour.
  • When it lifts off with its jetpack, run sideways: the landing hits a 4 block circle.
