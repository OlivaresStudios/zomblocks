The Zombot

BOSSCANNOT INFECT

A giant robot zombie, about 5 blocks tall. It slams the ground, sweeps everything in front of it and roars so hard that everyone around is blown away. Every 27 seconds it calls its team.

OverviewStrategyGallery

Attacks

Ground Slam

Smashes the floor 2.5 blocks in front of it. Everyone within 5 blocks is hit and thrown in the air.

14 dmg
Sweep

A wide swing (220°, 5.8 blocks) that hits every survivor in front of it at once and knocks them far back.

10 dmg
Roar

4 shock waves in a row: every survivor within 10 blocks is pushed away and the screen shakes. Used from up to 16 blocks.

push
Call the team

Calls 2 team members every 27 s (first call 5 s into the fight), never more than 6 around it.

x2

Armor

Its metal plates absorb 40% of every hit, but explosions deal 120% damage. Bring the Boom Launcher, Virus Bombs or an Explosive Virus Barrel.

Team

Angry Reader
Pole Vaulter
Linebacker
Ladder Zombie

Loot

Every player who hurt it gets their own loot: 20-30 scrap, 1 jackpot and 2 bonus items. 25% chance the weapon comes upgraded.

HOW TO BEAT IT
Deal with its team first, keep moving sideways to dodge the sweep, and save your explosives for it: they hit 3x harder than your melee weapons on its armor.
