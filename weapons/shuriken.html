Shuriken mods

Right click the Fabricator with shurikens in hand: the mod goes on up to 8 shurikens of the stack and works on every hit. The model does not change; the name and the description do, and a shuriken you pick up again keeps its mod.

Electric Shuriken

Every hit shocks 2 s, x2 if wet

Electric Cable + 2 scrap
Toxic Shuriken

Every hit poisons 5 s

Poison Leaves + 2 scrap
Fire Shuriken

Every hit sets on fire 4 s

Lighter + 2 scrap
